## A real application. A deliberate starting point.

You will build a coach that collects a resume and job description, asks behavioural and technical questions, and saves useful feedback. Start with the supplied Blazor and Aspire shell. Add the agent, give it tools, connect persistent data, and then split the work between specialists.

This workshop is for **C#/.NET developers who are new to agents**. You do not need prior experience with Agent Framework, MCP, or Foundry. You do need a local development environment and Azure model access.

<Architecture stage={5} />

## Understand the choice before writing the code

Every lesson begins with what you are adding and why it belongs in the application. Then you make specific edits in named files, run the result, and explain what happened. Hints and complete checkpoints are available without replacing your own work.

The site does not run your application or collect Azure credentials. Progress stays in your browser. Your code runs on your machine; your model runs in Foundry.
